THE IDEA BEHIND THE APP

About LeafGuard

A student project exploring how image classification can help people learn about crop-leaf health—thoughtfully, transparently, and with clear limits.

01

The challenge

Leaf symptoms can be difficult to recognize from a photo. This project explores ranking a leaf image against labels in a trained image-classification model.

02

The approach

A browser interface previews JPG and PNG images, applies MobileNetV2 image scaling, and displays the model’s top supported classes when compatible model files are provided.

03

The boundary

LeafGuard does not diagnose every plant disease, confirm the cause of a symptom, or recommend pesticide or treatment choices.

04

Privacy first

Images are decoded and processed in your browser. The page does not upload or persist images. Prediction history stays in page memory.
